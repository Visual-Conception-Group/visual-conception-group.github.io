## Ways to join

<div class="join-routes" markdown="1">

<section class="join-card" markdown="1">

### BTech students

IIIT Delhi BTech students can work with us through an independent project, an independent study
or a BTech project (BTP). Email us your CV, your grades and the topics you would like to work on,
ideally before the semester begins.

</section>

<section class="join-card" markdown="1">

### MTech students

IIIT Delhi MTech students, including MTech (Research), can do their thesis with us. Email us your
CV and research interests. To apply to the MTech programme itself, see IIIT Delhi's
[admissions page](https://www.iiitd.ac.in/admission).

</section>

<section class="join-card" markdown="1">

### PhD

PhD students are admitted through IIIT Delhi's
[PhD admission process](https://www.iiitd.ac.in/admission/phd). If you would like to work with
us, apply there and mention the group and your research interests in your statement of purpose.
You are welcome to email us before applying.

</section>

<section class="join-card" markdown="1">

### Research assistants

We hire research assistants on funded projects. Positions are listed under
[Open positions](#open-positions) below and in the [news]({{ '/allnews.html' | relative_url }}).
The stipend follows institute norms. Send your CV (PDF, at most 2 pages) with the subject
"RA application".

</section>

<section class="join-contact" markdown="1">

## Writing to us

Email Prof. A V Subramanyam at [{{ site.contact.email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact.email }}).
Say who you are, what you would like to work on and why, and attach your CV as a PDF. It helps to
read a few of our [recent papers]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) first and mention the ones
that interest you.

</section>
